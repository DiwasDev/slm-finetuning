PII Scrubbing & Git History Sanitization

Scrubbed all personal names, personal email addresses, phone numbers, and LinkedIn handles. Purged commit 132c1f2 from git history so zero commits ever held raw personal data. Replaced with RFC 2606 example emails (@example.com) and realistic business pseudonyms.

Synthetic Scale-Up via Claude 3.5 Sonnet

Initial 48 leads were insufficient to prevent ~3B SLM memorization. Generated 54 new high-fidelity synthetic B2B marketing agencies across 10 niches (SEO, Paid Media, Lifecycle, ABM, CRO, RevOps). Teacher model produced structured 4-field candidate drafts adhering to Noman's 22 rules.

+54 Diverse Agencies
3

Human Editorial Gate (Diwas In-the-Loop Review)

Every single lead reviewed through the interactive dashboard. Diwas personally revised 89 leads (87.3%) to inject his authentic voice and eliminate AI cliches, accepted 7 verbatim, and disqualified 6 leads, resulting in 96 high-signal training examples.

100% Gated by Diwas
4

Cryptographic Stratification (Train / Val / Test)

Split all leads into 82 Train (76 approved, 6 rejected withheld), 10 Validation, and 10 Test. Cryptographic audit verified zero leakage and mathematical disjointness: Train ∩ Val = ∅, Train ∩ Test = ∅, and Val ∩ Test = ∅.

Audited Zero-Leakage
5

SLM Supervised Fine-Tuning (~3B Parameters)

Exported clean JSONLs (slm_train.jsonl, slm_val.jsonl, slm_test.jsonl) with complete SHA-256 provenance on every training row, ready for LoRA fine-tuning on Qwen2.5-3B or Llama-3.2-3B.

The 4-Field Output Taxonomy (Noman's 22-Rule Framework)

Every single training example teaches the SLM to execute a strict 4-step diagnostic reasoning chain:

1. Operational Pain Point

Identifies 1 specific, non-obvious operational bottleneck or profit leak tied directly to their delivery scale or agency tools.

2. Personalization Angle

Cites verifiable public evidence (a client testimonial, a specific methodology name, or a recent podcast topic) to prove relevance.

3. Pattern-Interrupt Hook

Casual, lower/sentence-case subject line under 8 words directly addressing the operational problem with zero marketing fluff.

4. High-Reply Body Copy

Under 85 words, exactly 1 pain point, low-friction soft question CTA, signed off with Regards,\nDiwas.
